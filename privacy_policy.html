Official Privacy Policy & COPPA Compliance Statement

🛡️ Family & Children Certified

Effective Date: September 1, 2026
Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Welcome to Magic Kids Radio ("we", "our", or "us"). We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of children and their families. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices regarding our mobile application.

1. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Magic Kids Radio is designed for children, toddlers, preschoolers, and families. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and Google Play's Families Policy.

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do NOT collect, store, request, or share any personal information from children (e.g., names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, device IDs, or photos).
  • No User Accounts: The app does not require sign-up, registration, or logins.
  • No Targeted Advertising: The app contains no behavioral tracking, advertising SDKs, or third-party ad profiling.

2. Information Stored Locally on Device

To provide a seamless user experience, the app stores minimal non-personal preference data locally on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Favorite Rhymes: IDs of nursery rhymes added to the "Favorite Star Bank" so children can replay them.
  • Theme Preferences: Dark/Night mode setting.
  • Language Station Preference: Last selected language station (English, Hindi, or Marathi).

This data remains solely on your device and is never transmitted to any external server.

3. Third-Party Services & Media Streaming

Magic Kids Radio plays curated educational nursery rhymes and videos through secure, first-party web embeds (YouTube embed player in privacy-enhanced mode with youtube-nocookie.com):

  • No cookies or behavioral ad tracking is enabled for children.
  • All media playback operates in child-safe, restricted mode.

4. Parental Gate & Safety Controls

Magic Kids Radio includes an interactive Parental Gate (math challenge / multi-step verification) that ensures child users cannot access adult settings or external links. Parents have full control over sleep timers and language filters.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard technical measures to ensure the app is secure, reliable, and completely safe for young children.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
